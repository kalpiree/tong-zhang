Tong Zhang

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Technology Sydney, supervised by Guandong Xu and Xianzhi Wang. My research focuses on how recommender systems use large language models to retrieve relevant items, adapt to changing user preferences, and give overlooked items a fair chance of being shown. I also collaborate with Upwork on ranking freelancers for job searches.

I am seeking postdoctoral and lecturer positions in machine learning, information retrieval, and recommender systems.

Selected work

All publications

FRAG: Fair Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Sequential Recommendation

Tong Zhang, Nitin Bisht, Zihao Li, Xianzhi Wang, Xiuwen Gong, Guandong Xu

WSDM 2027·Accepted for oral presentation

When some item groups receive too little exposure, FRAG adjusts which items the language model retrieves and considers for its next recommendation.

SCAU-REC: Subspace-Constrained Adapter Updates for LLM-Based Recommendations

Tong Zhang, Nitin Bisht, Chongjun Xia, Xianzhi Wang, Jing Long, Guandong Xu

CIKM 2026·Oral presentationPaper

As users’ preferences change, SCAU-REC updates a language model’s adapters within a restricted subspace to preserve what the recommender has already learned.

SarRec: Statistically-guaranteed Augmented Retrieval for Recommendation

Tong Zhang, Nitin Bisht, Zihao Li, Guandong Xu, Xianzhi Wang

CIKM 2025Paper

SarRec retrieves context for each user, then calibrates the model’s output to control the chance of leaving a relevant item out of the recommendation set.

ALIGN-Rec: Continual Recommendation under Heterogeneous Unlearning Requests

Nitin Bisht, Sumit Bisht, Tong Zhang, Yu Yang, Huan Huo, Guandong Xu

NeurIPS 2026Paper

ALIGN-Rec constrains future model updates to limit how much deleted interactions can influence a recommender as it continues learning.
